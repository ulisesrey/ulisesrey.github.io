I am a Senior Data Scientist with a PhD in Neuroscience, currently building advanced ML and AI solutions at Keepler Data Tech. I specialize in leveraging NLP and generative AI techniques to solve complex challenges.
Previously I worked as a researcher at the University of Vienna, where I explored the intersection of neuronal activity and behavior. My work involved single neuron whole-brain imaging of freely moving C. elegans, capturing the complexity of biological neural networks in real-time.
I earned my PhD at Freie Universität Berlin under Stephan Sigrist, supported by an IMPRS fellowship from the Max Planck Institute of Colloids and Interfaces, with co-supervision by Reinhard Lipowsky. Originally from Barcelona, I hold a degree in Human Biology from Universitat Pompeu Fabra, and a Master in Data Science from the Universitat Oberta de Catalunya.
